## Required enrichment before contact

For each approved target, add:

- Named agency owner, operations lead, delivery director, or technical lead.
- Public evidence that the person still holds the role.
- One recent project, service-page detail, hiring signal, or workload observation.
- The exact reason Dimaso is complementary rather than a direct substitute.
- Approved channel and personalized first sentence.
- Matching UTM campaign row.

Do not send the same template to all ten agencies. Start with three A-priority targets, learn from replies, then revise the message before the next batch.
