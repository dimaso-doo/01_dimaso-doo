## Acceptable proof when revenue data is unavailable

- Number of templates, URLs, products, roles, integrations, or migration redirects handled.
- Launch or takeover date and duration of ongoing support.
- Before/after error counts, response time, Core Web Vitals, uptime, or release frequency.
- Documented workflow reduction, recovery test, QA coverage, or publishing improvement.
- A direct client statement approving the scope and result.

Avoid “high volume,” “faster,” “improved,” or “reliable” unless the page explains what those terms mean and how they were verified.
